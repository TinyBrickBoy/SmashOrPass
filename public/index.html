Too many dead repos?
Smash or Pass.

Sign in with GitHub and swipe through your repos, oldest first. Smash keeps a repo, pass puts it on the pile to archive or delete. Nothing happens until you confirm at the end.

Sign in with GitHub

Required scopes: repo and delete_repo. GitHub's repo scope grants full access to all your public and private repos (and those of organizations you belong to); this app only uses it to list and archive repos you own. delete_repo is used only for repos you explicitly mark for deletion. Your token stays on the server in your session, is never stored in the browser and is revoked when you sign out.
